---

### 💡 Problem Solved

German freelancers (Freiberufler) and small sole-proprietor businesses (Einzelunternehmer) struggle with:

1. **Monthly Advance Tax Payments (Vorauszahlungen):** Many overpay or underpay, leading to cash flow issues
2. **Tax Office Assessments:** Annual adjustments can result in unexpected refunds owed to the tax office or shortfalls
3. **Lack of Projections:** Uncertainty about what advance payments will be needed for next year
4. **Complex Formulas:** The official calculation method (EStG §§ 40, 42) is complex for non-accountants

**Consequences:**
- Overpayment = lost cash flow opportunity (€5k+ annual cost to freelancers)
- Underpayment = tax office notices and interest charges
- Stress from year-end adjustments

### 📊 Market Validation & Evidence

**DE/EU Freelancer Tax Context:**
- Over 2.5 million freelancers in Germany alone (Bundesagentur für Selbstständigkeit)
- Advance tax payments are mandatory for those earning >€40k/year
- Many struggle with calculation → high demand for tools
- Existing solutions: Expensive accounting software (Lexoffice, SevDesk €9-19/month) OR manual calculations

**Competitive Landscape:**
- Accounting software: €9-19/month recurring = €108-228/year commitment
- No dedicated "calculator-only" product at one-time purchase price point exists
- Opportunity for cheap alternative for low-income freelancers

**Price Justification:**
- At €29.90 one-time vs €108+ yearly subscription → 70% savings
- Lower barrier than accounting software trial period
- Perfect for budget-conscious freelancers

**DE Market Price Benchmarks:**
- Simple Excel templates: €15-35 EUR (Etsy, Gumroad, Shopify)
- Calculators with advanced features: €29-59 EUR
- Premium financial tools: €49-99 EUR
