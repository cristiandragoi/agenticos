Outcome
Handler success now produces SUCCEEDED nodes/goals and EXECUTED_UNVERIFIED turns.
The controlled-file handler reports its comparison as a producer result, not
independent verification. Its outputs explicitly say verified:false. The kernel
no longer uses an Argus actor assertion as verification authority. Legacy turn
verifier booleans, compiled-plan success and ordinary replies cannot award VERIFIED.

The independent verification gate requires canonical goal/graph/node/operation/
attempt/worker identity, argument and resource hashes, a resolved versioned policy,
explicit approval or policy exemption reference, explicit postcondition, and
versioned, hashed, timestamped observer evidence with a finite validity window.
The host must supply a canonical-binding validator and trusted policy resolver;
missing trust callbacks deny promotion. Required human approval denies promotion
unless independently validated; this phase does not establish human approval.

Observers are registered by the trusted supervisor and must have both a different
actor and a different execution path from the producer. The gate invokes the
observer itself; caller-supplied handler success or an Argus label is insufficient.
The positive test uses a separate read of an actual temporary file and compares
its digest to the explicit postcondition. Proof objects cannot be structurally
forged, mutated or reused across goals.

Receipt and evidence lifecycle
Exact bindings are persisted. Receipts are one-use and expire. SQLite immediate
transactions serialize cancellation, observer reservation, evidence acceptance,
and promotion. Pending observers block promotion; generation checks reject stale
concurrent writers. Contradictory evidence poisons the receipt. Late evidence after
cancellation fails. Promotion rechecks complete evidence and current host binding.
Synchronous journal failure rolls promotion back; asynchronous commit callbacks
are rejected. Producer file-continuity receipts also enforce consumed-source
linkage, generation, expiry, exact operation identity and cancellation checks.
Producer receipts are never independent proof.

Remaining limits
No production observer, approval issuer or whole-goal proof-coverage adapter is
enrolled. Whole-goal COMPLETED remains fail closed; successful execution can be
reported as SUCCEEDED, and dependent actions cannot consume an unverified result.
Only presentation may consume a successful unverified dependency. The gate can
produce operation-level VERIFIED in isolated trusted-host tests. This does not

and promotion are blocked. Ordinary SQLite write access is not an authenticity
guarantee. Future host enrollment requires separate review.
