# EU Business Launch OS - Notion Template for German Solopreneurs

## Product Overview
A comprehensive Notion template system tailored for German/EU entrepreneurs launching digital businesses, e-commerce stores, and service-based ventures.

---

## Problem Solved
German solopreneurs and micro-SMEs struggle with:
- Lack of structured operational systems for business launch
- No centralized knowledge base for EU regulatory compliance
- Difficulty scaling from solo founder to small team
- Missing SOP templates for common business operations
- Unorganized customer communication workflows

---

## Target Customer
- **Primary**: German/EU solopreneurs, freelancers, micro-entrepreneurs (1-5 employees)
- **Secondary**: Startup founders in EU markets, digital nomads targeting European clients
- **Ideal Profile**: Individuals aged 25-45 with existing business ideas or early-stage ventures seeking operational structure
