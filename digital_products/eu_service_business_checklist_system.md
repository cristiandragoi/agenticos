# EU Service Business Checklist System - Digital Product Opportunity

## Product Overview
Comprehensive operational checklist and workflow system for service-based businesses (consulting, coaching, agencies) operating in the German/EU market.

---

## Problem Solved
EU service business owners struggle with:
- Inconsistent client deliverable quality
- Missed project milestones due to poor tracking
- Lack of standardized onboarding processes
- Client communication gaps leading to complaints
- Difficulty scaling from solo to team without operational chaos

## Product Components

### Core Checklist Systems:
1. **Client Onboarding SOP** - Welcome sequence, contract signing, kickoff procedure
2. **Project Initiation Checklist** - Scope definition, timeline setup, resource allocation
3. **Weekly Project Review Template** - Milestone tracking, risk assessment
4. **Quality Assurance Workflow** - Deliverable review process, client approval steps
5. **Client Communication Protocol** - Email templates, response time standards
6. **Invoice & Payment SOP** - Billing procedures, late payment handling
7. **Project Closure Checklist** - Handover, feedback collection, referral request
8. **Risk Management Framework** - Issue escalation, contingency planning

### Bonus Components:
- Email signature and communication templates (German/English)
- Client contract addendums for common scenarios
- Project timeline templates (Gantt chart formats)
- Team handover documentation standards
