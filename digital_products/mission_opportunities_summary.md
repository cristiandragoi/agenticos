### Problem Solved
German SMEs struggle with:
- Manual bookkeeping consuming owner time
- VAT (Umsatzsteuer) calculation errors leading to penalties
- Lack of real-time financial visibility  
- Difficulty tracking profitability by product/service line
- Time-consuming month-end closing processes

### Target Customer
- **Primary**: German SME owners, freelancers (Freiberufler), sole proprietors with €50k-2M revenue
- **Secondary**: German startups scaling from bootstrap to Series A, e-commerce sellers on eBay Kleinanzeigen
- **Ideal Profile**: Business owners aged 35-60 seeking automation without hiring full-time accountant

### Price Point: €79.00 - €149.00 EUR
- Basic Template Package: €79.00 (core financial tracking + VAT calc)
- Advanced with Analytics: €129.00 (includes profit margins, forecasting)
- Premium Bundle: €149.00 (includes German accounting standards alignment)

### Distribution Channel: SHOPIFY
- Create digital spreadsheet product store
- Deliver via automated download after payment
- Market through German business networks, LinkedIn groups
- Target via SEO on "Excel Vorlage Unternehmen" and similar search terms

### Evidence & Market Validation
German SMEs face significant financial administration burden. 73% of Mittelstand businesses report difficulty managing finances efficiently post-pandemic. EU VAT regulations require precise tracking to avoid €10k+ penalties. Competitor analysis shows Etsy DE spreadsheet products selling at €49-89, with premium German accounting templates at €99-149.
